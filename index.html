Dota 2 minigames

Browser recreations of the minigames from Dota 2, rebuilt from the game's own data files. Signing in is optional — everything is playable without it.

Pick the Lock

Catch the sweeping needle on each bar before the clock runs out. From the Dark Carnival event.
